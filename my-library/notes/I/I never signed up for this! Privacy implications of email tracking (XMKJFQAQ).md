# I never signed up for this! Privacy implications of email tracking

**Englehardt, Steven; Han, Jeffrey; Narayanan, Arvind**

## Abstract

Abstract We show that the simple act of viewing emails contains privacy pitfalls for the unwary. We assembled a corpus of commercial mailing-list emails, and find a network of hundreds of third parties that track email recipients via methods such as embedded pixels. About 30
