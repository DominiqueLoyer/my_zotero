# #knowledge #representation | Dominique Loyer

## Abstract

This paper presents a critical evaluation of
Yoav Shoham’s work, "Why Knowledge Rep-
resentation Matters" (Shoham, 2016), which
advocates for a revitalization of Knowledge
Representation (KR)—particularly its logical
and philosophical foundations—in the current
era of Artificial Intelligence (AI) dominated
by Machine Learning (ML).
